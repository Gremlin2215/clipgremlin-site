Looking for a Powder alternative?

Powder.gg shut down in mid-2026. Here's an honest look at what former Powder users can switch to, what each tool costs, and where each one falls short.

What happened to Powder?

Powder was a free-to-start AI clipping app for gamers and streamers. It ran locally on Windows, supported 40+ games, detected Twitch chat spikes, and built automatic montages. Around July 2026 Powder shut down and subscriptions were cancelled. Visiting powder.gg now redirects to the team's farewell note.

What former Powder users need in a replacement

  • Moment detection that understands streams. Chat spikes, reactions, and funny bits, not only kills.
  • Works with long VODs. 4–8 hour streams without burning through credits.
  • Vertical output. Facecam + gameplay framed for TikTok, Shorts, and Reels, with captions.
  • Low effort. Powder users liked that clipping happened mostly on its own.
  • Fair pricing. Powder had a free tier and a cheap Pro plan, so price matters.
