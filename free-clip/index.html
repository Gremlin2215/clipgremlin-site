FAQ

Fair questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. No card, no subscription, nothing to cancel. It's one clip so you can see the kind of edits we make.

Who edits the clip?

The ClipGremlin team edits every free clip by hand. ClipGremlin is pre-launch (waitlist only), so this is a preview of the style we're building.

What can I send?

A YouTube, Twitch VOD or Google Drive link to a stream or VOD under 1 hour. Make sure the link is public or viewable by anyone with the link. We don't take file uploads.

What if I don't know which moment to pick?

Leave the moment field blank and we'll find the best one for you.

Will you post my clip?

Only if you tick the repost box. Either way, the clip is yours to post wherever you like.
